Everything you need before the weather turns

Built for people who need to decide what to do, not just read numbers.

🌤️

Mobile weather forecast

Current conditions, hourly and 8-day forecast, feels-like temperature, humidity, wind, UV and sunrise.

🛟

My Area Safety

A daily risk meter with plain advice, plus a map of shelters, hospitals and water points.

🔮

What-if simulator

Try more heat, rain or sea-level rise and see which wards become high risk first.

🔔

Personal alerts

Set your home type, age group and job to get warnings that fit you, by push, SMS, WhatsApp or voice.

📸

Citizen reports

Report waterlogging or damage with photo, location and voice. Neighbours confirm it, so reports stay trustworthy.

🗣️

Ask in your language

Question box for Hindi, Marathi and English, plus an offline pack and emergency numbers.

How it works

From raw data to a clear action in three steps.

Collect

Weather from IMD or Open-Meteo, satellite and land-use layers, census data and citizen reports.

Analyse

Trend analysis, flood and heat models, and a vulnerability index for every ward.

Act

Alerts, safe places and adaptation tips such as drain cleaning, shade trees and water storage.
